Data flow, step by step

  1. Sensors give raw values to the ESP32.
  2. Firmware filters them and checks thresholds, fall and SOS logic.
  3. Normal data goes to the live node; a confirmed event also goes to the events list.
  4. Buzzer sounds locally on an event.
  5. Firebase pushes the change to the dashboard.
  6. Caretakers receive the alert.

Firebase data structure

patients/
  p1/
    live/    pulse, spo2, temp, svm, ts
    events/  type (FALL | SOS | ...), ts
    history/ timestamped readings

Power: 18650 Li-ion cell charged through a TP4056 module. Prototype only, not a certified medical device.
